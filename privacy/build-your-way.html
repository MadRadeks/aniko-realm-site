Build Your Way: Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Current Android offline build

Build Your Way: Habit Tracker is published by Aniko Realm, an independent app publisher based in Serbia. This notice describes the current Android offline build. It does not yet describe a released iOS build; before any iOS version is distributed, we will verify its actual data handling and update this notice as needed. Privacy questions can be sent to anikorealm@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

You may enter habit names, schedules, check-ins, streak history, optional notes or reasons, pauses, archives, reminders and preferences. The Android build described here stores this information in the app's private local storage using a SQLite database. Ordinary habit tracking does not require an account or an Internet connection.

The current build does not intentionally transmit your habit records or check-in history to an Aniko Realm cloud service. We do not have remote access to the habit history stored on your device.

2. How local information is used

Information stored on your device is used to display habits and history, calculate statistics and streaks, schedule optional reminders, populate selected home-screen widgets, and create progress previews or images when you request them.

3. Reminders and home-screen widgets

If you enable reminders, the Android device schedules local notifications. Reminder text is intended to be generic and not to display private habit notes on the lock screen. Android permissions, device settings and power-management behavior can affect delivery. Widgets may show the habit details you select to anyone who can see your device's home screen.

4. Photos, sharing and exported files

If you choose a photograph to create a progress image, the current Android implementation processes it locally. A habit name is excluded from the image by default unless you choose to include it. If you save or share an image or file, it is sent to the destination or person you choose; that recipient or service may process it under its own privacy terms.

Any copy you export, save or share is outside the app's local-data deletion controls. You are responsible for removing those copies from the destinations where you placed them.

5. Backup and payments

Manual JSON backup and restoration are planned for a future verified Pro version and are not an active paid service in the current offline Android build. The current build does not have live Google Play subscription checkout or a purchase-verification service operated by Aniko Realm. If a later release adds payments, purchase verification, synchronization or another online feature, this notice and the store privacy disclosures will be updated to describe the actual data flows before that version is distributed.

6. Ads, analytics and app components

In the Android source reviewed for this notice, we did not identify a separately configured advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting service. Android, Capacitor and app plugins provide device features such as local storage, notifications, file access, media selection and sharing. This source-level review is not a substitute for checking the exact release package and all included SDKs; store disclosures must match the version actually distributed.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Your habit information remains in the app's local storage until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The Android build reviewed for this notice disables automatic Android app-data backup. The app includes an advanced local-data deletion control in Settings. If you use it, clear storage or uninstall the app, locally stored habit history may be permanently lost. Backups or files you previously saved or shared must be deleted separately from their destinations.

The app uses private Android app storage and operating-system protections, but no storage method can eliminate every risk. Keep your device secure and be mindful of widgets, notifications and exported files. This version does not provide account creation or an Aniko Realm cloud account, so there is no app account or cloud habit history to delete.

8. Support email and this website

If you email anikorealm@gmail.com, Google processes the message through Gmail. We receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to support requests, investigate reported issues and keep necessary correspondence. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence; some records may need to be retained where required by law.

This static website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The site does not include publisher-added advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical and website-usage data, such as IP address, browser or device information, request time, referring page and pages requested, to provide and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

For information about how Google handles data through Gmail, see the Google Privacy Policy. The privacy practices of these providers are governed by their own notices and services.

9. Privacy requests and policy changes

For personal information held by Aniko Realm, such as support correspondence, contact anikorealm@gmail.com. Habit information stored only on your device is not accessible to us and must be deleted from that device by you. For technical data processed by GitHub or Google through their services, please consult the relevant provider's privacy notice.

Build Your Way: Habit Tracker is a general-purpose habit tracker, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service. If features or data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant version.

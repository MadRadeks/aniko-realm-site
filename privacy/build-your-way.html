Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Android offline version

Build Your Way Out (also shown as Build Your Way) is a habit-tracking app published under the name Aniko Realm, an independent app publisher based in Serbia. Questions about privacy can be sent to anikorealm@gmail.com.

1. Habit information stored on your device

You can add habits, schedules, check-ins, streak history, optional notes or reasons, reminders, pauses, archives and preferences. The Android implementation described here stores habit progress and preferences in the app's private local storage, using a native SQLite database. Ordinary habit tracking works without an Aniko Realm account or an Internet connection.

The current offline implementation does not intentionally send your habits or check-in history to an Aniko Realm cloud database. If later versions introduce paid purchase verification or other online services, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

2. How the information is used

Your locally stored information is used to display your habits, calculate statistics and streaks, show your history, schedule optional reminders and create previews or progress images when you request them.

3. Reminders and home-screen widgets

If you enable reminders, the Android device schedules local notifications. Reminder messages are designed to be generic and not display your private habit notes on the lock screen. Notification permissions and delivery timing are controlled partly by Android. Home-screen widgets may show the habit details you selected to anyone who can view your device's home screen.

4. Optional photos, image sharing and exports

If you select a photograph to create a progress image, the app processes it locally. By default, a habit name is excluded from the shared progress image unless you choose to include it. If you choose to save or share an image, the file goes to the app, location or person you select. The selected receiving service may process the file under its own privacy rules.

Manual JSON backup or restoration is planned for a verified Pro version and is not an active paid service in the current offline Android build. If this becomes available, any backup you choose to save or share will be your responsibility to protect.

5. Payments and future Pro features

The current offline Android build has no live Google Play subscription checkout. If a future version enables paid subscriptions, Google Play will process checkout and a separate verification system may process purchase tokens, subscription status and installation-related identifiers. Before such a version is released, this policy will be revised to explain which data is transmitted, which providers receive it, the purpose, retention and deletion choices.

6. Ads, analytics and third-party components

The Android source reviewed for this notice does not intentionally include a separately configured advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting service. The app uses Android and Capacitor components for device storage, reminders, photos, filesystem access and sharing. The final released version and included third-party components will be reviewed again before publication.

7. Data retention, deletion and security

Your habit history and preferences are kept locally until you remove them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, except for copies you have saved or shared independently. The Android build under review disables automatic Android app-data backup. The app provides an advanced action for removing local data, and Android also provides app data-clearing controls. Files you previously exported or shared must be removed separately from their destinations.

The app uses private Android app storage and operating-system permissions, but no storage method can eliminate all risk. Be mindful of who can see your widgets, notifications and exported files. The current version does not provide an APP2 account, so there is no separate APP2 cloud account to delete.

8. Support communications and this website

When you email us for support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, handle a reported issue and keep necessary correspondence; messages are retained only as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by the email service provider. Please avoid including sensitive notes or complete backups in support emails.

This website does not include Aniko Realm advertising or analytics scripts. Its web-hosting provider may process technical request data in accordance with that provider's policies.

9. Intended use and policy updates

Build Your Way is a general-purpose habit tracker, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service. This notice describes the currently reviewed offline Android implementation. If app features or data-handling practices change, the policy will be updated with a new date.

Frequently asked questions

Why can I only add two active habits?

The Free version allows two active habits. Archiving a habit makes room for another while keeping its recorded history. Paused habits still count toward the active limit.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Ordinary habit tracking and history in the current Android version are stored locally. No sign-in is required.

Does history synchronize to my other devices?

No automatic cross-device synchronization is included in the current build. Manual backup and restore are planned for a future Pro version and are not available as a live paid service in this build.

Why did a reminder appear late?

Notification permission, quiet hours, power saving and Android's inexact notification scheduling can all affect timing. Review your reminder settings and the phone's notification permissions.

Can I review or correct an earlier day?

Yes. Use the app's Review a day control to view historical entries and confirm an available change.

Can I share my progress?

You can create a progress image, choose a theme and share or save it using your device's controls. Habit names are excluded by default unless you choose otherwise.

How can I delete locally stored habit data?

Use the advanced local-data deletion control in Settings, or clear the app's storage using Android settings. Uninstalling the app can also remove local history. Any files you independently exported or shared must be deleted from their destinations separately.
